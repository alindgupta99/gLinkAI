AI & automation for real estate agents and builders

Most enquiries come in after office hours. Most leads aren't serious. Most agents are too busy on site visits to follow up. Here's how AI fixes all three — in plain English.

The honest truth about real estate enquiries

You get 50 WhatsApp messages a day. 40 are time-wasters. 10 are real — and by the time you call back, 6 have moved on. The problem isn't lack of leads. It's too many low-quality leads and zero time to qualify them.

🏠 The unpaid intern analogy

Think of AI as an unpaid intern who picks up every WhatsApp, every Magicbricks ping, every site enquiry. They politely ask the buyer 4 quick questions — budget, location, timeline, loan or cash — and only forward the serious ones to you. The time-wasters get a polite "we'll check and revert" automatically.

Two simple AI use cases to start with

Use Case 1 · Start here

WhatsApp property enquiry bot

Connect your business WhatsApp to an AI that knows your active listings. It answers "any 3BHK in Whitefield?", shares photos and price, asks the buyer's budget and timeline, and only pings you when the lead is serious.
