AI & automation for CAs, tax consultants & advisors

Filing season chaos. Chasing clients for documents. Same questions about GST, ITR, PAN every week. Here are two AI use cases that quietly remove the grind — without changing how you advise.

The grind no CA talks about

You spend 30% of your time chasing clients for invoices, bank statements, and Form 16s. Another 20% answering "Sir, last date kab hai?" on WhatsApp. The actual advisory work — the thing clients pay you for — gets squeezed into evenings.

📊 The articleship analogy

Remember articleship? AI is like having three permanent articles who never quit, work all night during filing season, never forget a deadline, and politely chase clients for documents without making it awkward. You sign off on the work; they do the donkey-running.

Two simple AI use cases to start with

Use Case 1 · Start here

Automated document collection & reminders

Each client gets a private WhatsApp link. The bot asks them for the documents you need ("share GST sales summary", "share bank statement for Apr-Sep"), checks them off as they come in, and follows up automatically if anything is missing.

Before
  • You/your articles call 30 clients before each filing
  • Documents lost in WhatsApp chats
  • Last-minute scramble on filing day
➜
After
  • Clients receive a polite checklist automatically
  • Documents auto-sorted by client and type
  • Filing day: most files ready 3 days early
Use Case 2 · Advisory

AI WhatsApp assistant for client FAQs

Clients ask their basic questions — "GST return ki last date?", "ITR file kar diya?", "TDS certificate kahan se download karein?" — to your branded AI assistant. It replies instantly in their language with accurate, current information. Only complex questions reach you.

Before
  • 50+ basic WhatsApp messages every day
  • Same answer copy-pasted 20× a week
  • Real advisory work happens after 9 pm
➜
After
  • Bot handles 80% of routine queries
  • Your phone goes quiet during work hours
  • Advisory work happens during the day
