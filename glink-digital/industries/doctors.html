AI & automation for doctors and clinics

Spend less time on phone calls, appointment chaos, and chasing reviews — and more time with patients. Two simple AI use cases to start with this month.

The reality of running a small clinic today

Patients message at 10 pm. The front-desk staff answers the same five questions 40 times a day. No-shows are 25–30%. New patients can't find your clinic on Google. You wanted to be a doctor — not a call-centre manager.

🩺 The unhired receptionist analogy

Think of AI as the receptionist you didn't hire — works night shifts, never gets tired, speaks Hindi and English, and never forgets to remind a patient about their follow-up. You don't replace your staff; you give them superpowers.

Two simple AI use cases to start with

Use Case 1 · Start here

WhatsApp appointment booking

A patient WhatsApps your clinic number. An AI bot greets them in Hindi or English, shows available slots, books the appointment, and adds it to your calendar — all in under a minute, any time of day.

See it live → A working doctor site we built with online booking, WhatsApp consults & UPI payments.

Before

Use Case 2 · Reputation

Automatic Google review collection

The day after a visit, the system sends a friendly WhatsApp asking the patient how it went. If they're happy, it gives them a one-tap link to the Google review page. If they're not, the message goes to you privately.

Before
  • 5–10 Google reviews after years of practice
  • Negative reviews surface first
  • New patients pick someone else
➜
After
  • 50+ genuine 5-star reviews in 90 days
  • Unhappy patients reach you before Google
  • Page-1 ranking on "doctor near me" searches
